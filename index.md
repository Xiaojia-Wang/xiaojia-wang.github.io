I work broadly on fundamental problems related to fluid mechanics. I obtained my PhD in Mechanical Engineering from Purdue Unversity, West Lafayette in 2022.  My doctoral research (under the supervison of Prof. Ivan C. Christov) is focused on the [fluid--structure interactions (FSIs) of microscale flows in compliant conduits](research.md). After my PhD, I became a Postdoctoral Assistant Professor in the Department of Mathematics at University of Michigan, Ann Arbor, and worked with Professor Silas Alben on flow dynamics and heat transfer of oscillating bodies.
